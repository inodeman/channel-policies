Privacy Policy

México uploader · Last updated: October 29, 2026

México uploader (the "App") is a private tool operated by Jaime Enriquez (the "Owner"), who owns the YouTube channel México y sus Secretos. This policy explains what the App does and how it handles data.

What the App does

The App is used only by the Owner to upload and manage videos on the Owner's own YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API v3 for this purpose only. The App is not offered to the public and is not used to access any other person's YouTube account or channel.

Information accessed

When the Owner authorizes the App through Google OAuth, the App can access the Owner's own YouTube channel information, the Owner's videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, privacy settings), and to upload new videos to it. Authorization tokens are stored locally on the Owner's own computer, kept private, and used only to perform these actions.

No collection of user data

The App does not collect, store, or process personal data from viewers, subscribers, or any other users.

Use and sharing

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the App's upload and video management features for the Owner's own channel. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, or used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose. The App does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized machine-learning or AI models, and no humans read the data except as needed for security or to comply with law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

By using YouTube API Services through the App, the Owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the App can be reviewed and revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention and deletion

The App keeps authorization tokens only as long as needed to operate. Revoking access in your Google Account invalidates them. To ask about or request deletion of any stored data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its date.
